# HSKK Exam Syllabus

## Traditional HSKK Question Types, Preparation Time and Answering Time

Traditional HSKK has three recorded speaking exams. Each has a maximum of 100 points and a passing score of 60. The timings below follow CTI's traditional HSKK specification. The numbered HSK 3.0 speaking exams have separate formats.

| Level | Tasks in order (items, section minutes) | Total items | Preparation | Answering sections | Approx. total | Passing score |
| --- | --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| [Elementary 初级](https://hskboost.com/en/exams/hskk/hskk-elementary/) | Listen and repeat (15, 4 min), listen and answer (10, 3 min), answer printed questions (2, 3 min) | 27 | 7 min | 10 min | 17 min | 60/100 |
| [Intermediate 中级](https://hskboost.com/en/exams/hskk/hskk-intermediate/) | Listen and repeat (10, 3 min), describe pictures (2, 4 min), answer printed questions (2, 4 min) | 14 | 10 min | 11 min | 21 min | 60/100 |
| [Advanced 高级](https://hskboost.com/en/exams/hskk/hskk-advanced/) | Listen and retell (3, 7 min), read aloud (1, 2 min), answer printed questions (2, 5 min) | 6 | 10 min | 14 min | 24 min | 60/100 |

Section times include any playback within the section. They show the scheduled answering block, including listening time where applicable.

[CTI traditional HSKK format and score reporting](https://admin.chinesetest.cn/gonewcontent.do?id=40576416)

## HSKK Scoring Criteria by Question Type

CTI's official self-assessment guide describes how performance is judged for each question type.

| Question type | HSKK levels | Criteria summarized from CTI's self-assessment guide |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Listen and repeat | Elementary, Intermediate | Accuracy and completeness of the repeated sentence |
| Listen and answer | Elementary | Correctness, concision and clarity |
| Describe pictures | Intermediate | Relevance to the picture, fluency and coherence |
| Listen and retell | Advanced | Coverage of main ideas, fidelity to the recording and fluency |
| Read aloud | Advanced | Pronunciation, intonation and fluency. Misread words, repetition and pauses affect the rating. |
| Answer printed questions | Elementary, Intermediate, Advanced | Relevance, amount of information and coherence. The guide also considers fluency, pauses, repetition and grammatical errors. |
