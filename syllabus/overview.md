# HSKK Exam Syllabus

## HSKK Levels, Question Structure, Duration and Scoring

HSKK has three speaking exams. Each is scored out of 100 and has a passing score of 60.

| Level or paper | Question structure | Items | Total minutes | Scoring and reported skills |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| [Elementary](https://hskboost.com/en/exams/hskk/hskk-elementary/) | Listen and repeat (15), listen and answer (10), answer questions (2) | 27 | 20, including 7 for preparation | Overall speaking score. 60/100 to pass. |
| [Intermediate](https://hskboost.com/en/exams/hskk/hskk-intermediate/) | Listen and repeat (10), describe pictures (2), answer questions (2) | 14 | 23, including 10 for preparation | Overall speaking score. 60/100 to pass. |
| [Advanced](https://hskboost.com/en/exams/hskk/hskk-advanced/) | Listen and retell (3), read aloud (1), answer questions (2) | 6 | 25, including 10 for preparation | Overall speaking score. 60/100 to pass. |

[Official HSKK exam specification](https://www.chinesetest.cn/HSKK)
