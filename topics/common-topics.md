# Common HSKK Speaking Topics and Chinese Keywords by Level

This HSKBoost study index groups reusable speaking topics by level. The categories are for practice and carry no official frequency ranking.

## HSKK Elementary Speaking Topics · 初级

Elementary practice focuses on familiar people, places and routines.

| Topic | 话题分类 | Core Chinese keywords |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Personal information and family | 个人信息与家庭 | 名字、年龄、家人、爸爸、妈妈、住 |
| Daily routine and schedules | 日常作息与时间 | 起床、上课、上班、几点、今天、晚上 |
| Food and shopping | 饮食与购物 | 饭、面条、水果、买、多少钱、便宜 |
| Hobbies and weekends | 爱好与周末 | 喜欢、运动、看书、听音乐、周末、一起 |
| Places and transport | 地点与交通 | 学校、商店、医院、地铁、走路、怎么去 |
| Weather and seasons | 天气与季节 | 天气、晴、下雨、冷、热、夏天 |

## HSKK Intermediate Speaking Topics · 中级

Intermediate practice uses observable details for picture description and reasons or examples for spoken answers.

| Topic | 话题分类 | Core Chinese keywords |
| --- | --- | --- |
| People and actions in a picture | 人物与动作 | 左边、右边、旁边、正在、拿着、排队 |
| School and work | 学习与工作 | 作业、复习、计划、会议、合作、完成 |
| Travel and changes of plan | 出行与计划变化 | 出发、到达、路线、预订、改期、耽误 |
| Exercise and habits | 运动与生活习惯 | 锻炼、休息、饮食、坚持、习惯、身体 |
| Online and in-store shopping | 网购与线下消费 | 比较、价格、质量、方便、退货、选择 |
| Events and relationships | 活动与人际交往 | 邀请、约定、帮忙、误会、解释、感谢 |

## HSKK Advanced Speaking Topics · 高级

Advanced practice connects the main points of a spoken passage and develops an opinion with reasons, examples or trade-offs.

| Topic | 话题分类 | Core Chinese keywords |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Education and career choices | 教育与职业选择 | 能力、经验、机会、兴趣、就业、长期发展 |
| Technology and information | 科技与信息 | 效率、隐私、依赖、信息来源、辨别、影响 |
| Environment and urban life | 环境与城市生活 | 公共交通、垃圾分类、资源、成本、便利、改善 |
| Cultural exchange | 文化交流 | 习俗、背景、差异、适应、误解、沟通 |
| Work and personal responsibilities | 工作与个人责任 | 压力、分工、责任、优先顺序、边界、平衡 |
| Retelling and argument structure | 复述与观点组织 | 起因、经过、转折、结果、证据、因此 |
